About Mohamed Abdirahman Ahmed

Mohamed Abdirahman Ahmed is a Somali actor, screenwriter, politician and entrepreneur. He is interested in storytelling, filmmaking, public service, leadership, business, technology and community development.

In the business sector, he serves as Operations Manager at Midrar Properties, where he is involved in managing operations and supporting the company's growth and development.

He is also a member of Himilo Qaran, a political party in Somalia, with an interest in political affairs, youth empowerment and public service.
