About Me

Creativity, leadership and purpose.

Mohamed Abdirahman Ahmed is a multidisciplinary professional whose work brings together creativity, leadership, entrepreneurship, and a commitment to meaningful impact. As an Actor, Screenwriter, Director, and Photographer, he uses storytelling and visual creativity to communicate ideas, inspire audiences, and bring compelling stories to life.

Beyond the creative field, Mohamed is an Entrepreneur, Operations Manager, and Politician, combining strategic thinking, organizational leadership, and a strong sense of purpose. His journey reflects a belief that creativity and leadership can work together to create lasting value, build opportunities, and make a positive difference in society.
